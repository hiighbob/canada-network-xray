Reading the map

Buildings matter: separately branded facilities may occupy one carrier hotel. Gravity counts only this HE footprint, not all tenants.

Routes are not coverage: carrier lines are city-to-city abstractions from public materials, not surveyed ducts, guaranteed paths or live BGP routes.

Packets choose policy, not distance: Route Passport turns a traceroute into a visible multi-carrier journey.
