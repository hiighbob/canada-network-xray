What this reveals

Buildings matter. Several separately branded facilities can occupy the same carrier hotel. The gravity layer counts only the supplied HE footprint, not every tenant.

Routes are not coverage. Carrier lines are intentionally schematic city-to-city corridors reconstructed from public network maps; they are not surveyed ducts, guaranteed paths or current BGP routes.

Packets choose policy, not distance. Use Route Passport with traceroute coordinates, or compare paths in CIRA’s Canadian Traceroute Database.
